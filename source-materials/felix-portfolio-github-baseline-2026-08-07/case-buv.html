Creative problem

How do you make Data Science and AI feel human before it feels technical?

I decoded the recruitment brief, developed the central concept and translated it into a pitchable film structure rather than relying on generic technology claims.

The Futuristic Wave: Lead The Wave of Future Innovation.

The wave metaphor connected technological change with a student's ability to participate in and shape that change.

Production thinking

The idea was built to move.

Nonlinear story

Multiple environments connect through a common visual logic rather than a conventional campus tour.

Match cuts

Transitions were designed as part of the concept, allowing technical fields to flow into one another.

Scene planning

Twenty-one scenes specify camera direction, text, VFX, voice-over and location requirements.

Pitch readiness

The concept, script and proposal were developed as one integrated client-facing system.
