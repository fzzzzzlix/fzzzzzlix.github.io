About Felix

Curious enough to ask why. Practical enough to make it work.

Felix Phan is a creative strategist and storyteller who finds the tension inside a brief, gives it structure and stays involved until the idea can operate in the real world.

“I am most useful when the brief is exciting, slightly inconvenient and too human for a tidy answer.”

Felix moves meaningful ideas from insight, to story, to impact through research, writing, production and systems that survive the handover.

The range across creative development, strategic research, production, events and organisational systems is intentional. The same idea becomes stronger when the person shaping it understands how it will be pitched, produced, experienced, measured and handed over.

The work begins with evidence, but it does not end with a spreadsheet. Research becomes a human tension. The tension becomes a structure. The structure becomes a script, treatment, plan, event or operating system. Then the result is described with its limitations still attached.
