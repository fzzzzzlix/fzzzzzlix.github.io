## Tech

## Content rules

- Project IDs and role ownership follow the current approved portfolio master.
- Academic and speculative work stays labelled as such.
- Proposed budgets, KPIs and activations are not presented as realised outcomes.
- Confidential EMPACTS internal documents are not published.
- Do not use em dashes in body copy.
