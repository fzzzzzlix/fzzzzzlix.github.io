The contradiction

One platform was winning. The brand was not.

The research identified a gap between TRESemmé's TikTok momentum and weaker performance across Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. The strategic interpretation was not simply an awareness problem, but a trust-transfer problem.

Strategic response

Turn the salon claim into visible proof.

The team proposal connected influencer collaboration, interactive DOOH, a UGC challenge and offline pop-up experience into one media system. Budget, reach and KPI figures remain clearly labelled as proposed planning values.
