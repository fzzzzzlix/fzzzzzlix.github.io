# P35 StarX image slots

Eleven slots, all pending. Drop the file here, then set `src: asset("/images/cases/p35/<file>")`
on the matching slot in `app/content/cases/starx.ts`. Until then the page renders a labelled
placeholder carrying the reference, what the slot needs and its priority.

If a slot cannot be filled, delete the slot and its `<CaseSlot>` line rather than
substituting something generic.

| Ref | What it needs to be | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| IMG-01 | Hero. Wide frame from Map 01 with people in it, mid-action rather than posed. | Essential |
| IMG-02 | The League format: a team in their roles, or the scoring board being filled in. | High |
| IMG-03 | Mapping in play: a team searching, or the board they drew their route on. | Essential |
| IMG-04 | Map 01 establishing shot: the resort, the zones, or the group on arrival. | High |
| IMG-05 | The venue map or zone plan worked from during the recce. | Medium |
| IMG-06 | Nam Cát Tiên: moodboard, location reference, or a Tái Hoang Dã design-document page. | Medium |
| IMG-07 | Sunday We Vibe on the day: court, players, or the sealed strategy board handover. | Essential |
| IMG-08 | A page from the EP01 format workbook or script. Blur or crop anything not for publication. | High |
| IMG-09 | Đêm Tiệc Mặt Nạ: case board, character card, or table-setting mock-up. | Medium |
| IMG-10 | The StarX logo, on the dark ground, at size. | Essential |
| IMG-11 | Brand guidelines spread: the colour system page or the typography page. | High |

Never copy anything from the StarX archive folder into this repo: it contains account
credentials and participant phone numbers.
